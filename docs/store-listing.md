## English (en‑CA)

**Subtitle (30 chars)**: Cleaning, moving, painting

**Short description (80 chars, Google)**: Free quote in 2 minutes. Montréal, Laval, South & North Shore.

**Description**

Your space, in good hands.

Groupe 548 brings residential and commercial cleaning, post‑construction cleaning, window cleaning, moving, transport and interior painting under one roof — one bilingual team, 7 days a week, across Montréal, Laval, Longueuil, Brossard and both shores.

With the app:
• Describe what you need in 2 minutes, no account required
